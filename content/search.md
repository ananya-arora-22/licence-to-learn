Every reply institutes sent us, searchable by what is written inside it rather
than by institute name alone. Scanned replies are included: they have been run
through OCR, so a word that only ever appears as an image in a photocopied
annexure is still findable.

Matches are typo-tolerant, which matters here because OCR is imperfect. Each
result opens the original PDF at the page the match was found on. Nothing is
uploaded anywhere and no query leaves your browser — the search runs entirely
on your machine.

Email addresses and phone numbers are left out of the search text. They remain
in the original PDFs, which are linked unaltered.
